The uncomfortable truth

Most agent pilots never reach production.

Not because the models aren’t good enough. Because nobody owns the space between the business case and the code: which outcome, which capability, which data, who signs off.

Skip that step and you get a clever demo, a stack of tools, and an opex line nobody can explain. We start from the value, then build.
